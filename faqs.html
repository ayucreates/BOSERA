FAQs

7-day hassle-free exchanges & refunds.

How do I place an order?
Browse the collection, tap Add To Cart, then head to Cart and proceed to Checkout. Enter your delivery details and confirm your order.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI. More options coming soon.
How long does shipping take?
Orders are dispatched within 2–3 business days. Delivery takes 4–7 business days across India. See the Shipping Policy.
How do I track my order?
Use your Order ID on the Track Order page. You'll get your Order ID right after checkout.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 7-day exchange policy on all items, unused with tags attached. Full details on the Return Policy page.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes — before dispatch. Reach out on Support with your Order ID and we'll cancel & refund it.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently we ship within India only. We'll be expanding soon — join our newsletter to be the first to know.
