Return Policy

7-day hassle-free exchanges & refunds.

We want you to love your BOSERA fits. If something isn't right, we've got a simple 7-day exchange & return policy.

Returns & Exchanges

  • You can request an exchange or return within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in original packaging with tags attached.
  • Exchanges are subject to size availability.
  • To start a return, reach out on Support with your Order ID.

Refunds

  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the returned item.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method — UPI, card, or net banking refunds may take 2–5 extra days to reflect.
  • COD orders are refunded via UPI or bank transfer once your details are confirmed.

Non-Returnable Items

Undergarments, socks, and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned for hygiene and clearance reasons.
